What happens in the first 24 to 72 hours

1

Stop the source

Close the main water valve or the fixture valve. If the water is from rain or outdoors, note where it entered.

2

Call dispatch

Describe the rooms, the water type and the floors. We decide on pumps, extraction units and drying equipment.

3

Remove standing water

Extraction comes first, because every hour in water spreads moisture into walls and subfloors.

4

Measure and dry

Moisture meters map what is wet. Air movers and dehumidifiers then run until readings return to normal.

Drying usually takes several days, and the crew checks readings along the way. If you want the full method, our structural drying and dehumidification page explains equipment, readings and when materials come out. Water damage repair after the drying is done

Drying and repair are two phases. Once readings show the structure is dry, damaged drywall, insulation, flooring and trim are replaced or repaired. Some owners hire the same company for that, and others bring in a builder. Either way, a clear written scope keeps the two phases from overlapping or leaving gaps.

Walls, floors and ceilings each have their own repair story. A stained ceiling is covered on our ceiling water damage repair page, wet baseboards and drywall on our wall and drywall page, and carpet and pad on our carpet water damage page. Hardwood has its own risks, since boards can cup and crown, and our hardwood floor water damage page explains what to watch for.

Hollister conditions that shape water damage

Hollister has a dry summer and a wet season that runs through winter, so many leaks surface after the first heavy rains of the season. Roofs, gutters and old window seals that held up all summer can fail at once. Cold clear nights occasionally bring frost to the inland valley, and an exposed pipe in a garage or exterior wall can split when temperatures drop.

The older part of town adds its own mix. The Downtown Hollister Historic District and the Monterey Street Historic District, both listed on the National Register of Historic Places, hold buildings with older plumbing, plaster walls and wood floors that need a gentler drying plan. Homes in newer subdivisions on the east and north sides tend to show supply line, appliance and water heater leaks instead.

Two state routes shape travel. State Route 25 runs northwest toward the Bay Area and southeast toward Pinnacles, and State Route 156 runs west toward Monterey Bay. Dispatch uses both when a crew heads to Tres Pinos, San Juan Bautista or beyond. Hazel Hawkins Memorial Hospital, Hollister Municipal Airport and the Hollister Carnegie Library are familiar landmarks when you describe your location to dispatch.

What affects the cost of water damage restoration

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Water type. Clean water costs less to handle than gray or contaminated water.
  • Area and materials. Square footage, carpet, pad, hardwood and drywall all change the scope.
  • Equipment and days. More air movers and dehumidifiers, and more days of drying, raise the total.
  • Access. Crawl spaces, attics and multi-story homes take more time.
  • Rebuild. Drywall, flooring, paint and finish work are often scoped separately.

We do not publish exact prices because water losses vary too much. Call (831) 271-0459 and we explain the scope and price before work starts, so you can compare it with any other quote. A good estimate lists rooms, equipment, days and exclusions.

Questions about water damage in Hollister

How fast should I act after water damage in Hollister?

As soon as you are safe. Stop the water, call the 24/7 line and start photographing. Wet drywall, carpet pad and framing can begin to grow mold and swell within a couple of days, so early drying limits the repair that follows.

What does water damage restoration include?

It starts with removing standing water and measuring moisture. Then the crew sets drying equipment, removes materials that cannot be saved, cleans surfaces and checks readings until the structure is dry.

Rebuild such as new drywall, flooring and paint is repair work that follows drying. We explain scope and price before work starts.

How much does water damage restoration cost?

It depends on the water type, the area affected, the materials involved and how many days of drying are needed. We do not quote a number sight unseen. Call (831) 271-0459 and we explain the scope and price before work starts.

Will my homeowners insurance pay for water damage?

Many policies cover sudden events such as a burst pipe and exclude slow leaks or flooding. Your insurer decides coverage, so read your policy and call the insurer early. We keep drying logs and photographs that support a claim.

Do you serve both Hollister ZIP codes?

Yes. We cover 95023 for street addresses and 95024, which is a post office box ZIP, along with nearby 95075, 95045 and 95046 in Tres Pinos, San Juan Bautista and San Martin.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. Stores, offices, restaurants and warehouses in Hollister can call the same 24/7 line. See our commercial water damage page for how a business loss is scoped.

What should I do before the crew arrives?

Close the main water valve, switch off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and reachable, lift valuables off wet floors, and keep people and pets out of contaminated water. Then photograph the damage from a safe spot.

Can wet drywall and carpet always be saved?

No. Clean water caught early often allows drying in place. Water that is contaminated, or materials that stayed wet for days, are often removed. The crew decides with a moisture meter, not by eye.

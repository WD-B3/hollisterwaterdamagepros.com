Crawl Space Water Damage Cleanup in Hollister, CA

Water under the house soaks joists, subfloor and insulation. We remove it and dry the void from below.

Call (831) 271-0459

Crawl space water damage means water or excess moisture under a raised floor. It can come from a plumbing leak, a drainage problem, a high water table after heavy rain, or vapor rising from bare ground. Dispatch answers 24/7. The crawl space is not an emergency by the minute unless water is deep, but it is urgent because wood absorbs moisture for weeks.

If you see standing water, shut off the main water valve to rule out a plumbing source, stay out if the space has wiring or standing water near electrical lines, and call. We handle the water, the drying and the documentation, and a plumber or drainage contractor fixes the cause.

Signs of crawl space water damage

  • Standing water, mud or wet ground under the home.
  • Wet, sagging or detached insulation between floor joists.
  • Musty smells coming up through floor vents or from the floor.
  • Cupping or soft spots in flooring above, or doors that stick.
  • Dripping or sweating pipes and ducts in the space.

What lives under a raised floor

A crawl space holds floor joists, subfloor, insulation, pipes, ducts and sometimes wiring. Wet wood takes weeks to dry on its own, and wet insulation stops insulating and keeps wood damp. The space is dark, still and poorly ventilated, so moisture lingers.

If your home has a raised foundation, a plumbing leak can hide under the house while the floor above looks normal. That is why a stain, a smell or a sticking door is worth chasing, whatever the age of the house.

How we clean and dry a crawl space

1

Access and assess

The crew checks access, wiring hazards and the extent of wet materials with a moisture meter and flashlight or camera.

2

Extract water

Pumps and wet vacuums remove standing water, which can be slow in tight spaces.

3

Wet or fallen insulation is bagged out so joists can dry.

Honest note: restoration is not waterproofing

We remove water and dry the structure, but we do not install drains, sump pumps or vapor barriers. If water keeps coming back, you need a drainage or crawl space contractor to fix the cause, and a vapor barrier or encapsulation may be part of that. Ask the City of Hollister Development Services or San Benito County about permits for drainage work.

If water reached the floor above, see hardwood floor water damage repair for what happens to wood flooring. If the home sits on a slab instead, a slab leak is more likely, covered on our slab leak water damage page.

What to have ready when you call

  • Whether you can see standing water or only damp insulation.
  • Where the crawl space access is and whether it is clear.
  • Whether the plumbing is working normally.
  • Whether the water appeared after rain.
  • Whether the floor above feels soft, cupped or smells musty.

Crawl space jobs depend on access, so tell dispatch if the entry is in a closet, a hatch or an exterior door. Tight access changes the equipment and the time needed.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Crawling into a space with standing water and exposed wiring.
  • Leaving wet insulation in place because the surface looks fine.
  • Running a dehumidifier before the water source is stopped.

What affects crawl space cleanup cost

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Access. Tight or low access slows extraction and removal.
  • Water depth and area. More standing water means more pump time.
  • Insulation and materials. Wet insulation and damaged vapor barrier add steps.
  • Drying days. Joists and subfloor dry slowly in a closed space.
  • Cause correction. Plumbing or drainage repairs are separate trades.

Safety first and the standard we reference

Our approach to crawl space moisture draws on ANSI/IICRC S500 as a published reference for measurement and drying goals.

  • Do not enter a space with standing water and exposed wiring.
  • Wear a respirator-rated mask if you must enter a damp space.
  • Tell dispatch about any sewage smell.

Questions about Crawl Space Water Damage in Hollister

Is crawl space water damage an emergency?

It becomes one when water is deep, when a plumbing line is leaking, or when water has reached the floor above. Call dispatch, which is open 24/7, and we confirm availability for your address on the call.

Can you dry a crawl space without removing insulation?

Only if the insulation is dry. Wet insulation holds moisture against wood, so it is usually removed.

Will a dehumidifier alone fix a damp crawl space?

Not if water keeps coming in. Dry the structure first, then fix the source, which may be a plumber or drainage contractor.

Do I need a permit for crawl space work?

Drying usually does not, but drainage or structural work might. Ask City of Hollister Development Services, or the county if you are outside city limits.

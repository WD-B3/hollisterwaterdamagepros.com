Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Hollister, CA

Under-sink leaks, soaked base cabinets and wet kitchen floors need targeted drying before cabinets swell and fail.

Call (831) 271-0459

Kitchen water damage is usually found under the sink, behind a dishwasher or on the floor in front of the refrigerator. Cabinets are often made of particleboard, which swells and crumbles when wet, so drying quickly can save base cabinets that would otherwise be replaced. Dispatch answers 24/7.

If you find water, turn off the shutoff valves under the sink, unplug small appliances only from a dry spot and move dishes and food out of wet cabinets. This page covers the kitchen as a room. A failed washing machine or refrigerator line is also covered on appliance leak water damage.

Cabinets, countertops and floors

Solid wood cabinets can often be dried and kept, while particleboard cabinets swell and may need replacement. Toe kicks and the bottom shelf are the first to go. Stone and laminate countertops are usually fine, but the cabinet base that holds them up matters.

Flooring under cabinets is a hidden risk. Water can run under base cabinets and soak the subfloor without showing at the surface. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to check, and when needed we remove the toe kick or a small section to dry the cavity.

Kitchen water damage: our process

1

Stop and extract

The supply is off, water is extracted from the floor and from inside cabinets.

2

Map moisture

Meters check cabinets, flooring, walls and the area under the sink.

3

Remove what cannot dry

Swollen particleboard panels and saturated flooring are removed, and salvageable cabinets are dried in place.

4

Dry and verify

Air movers and a dehumidifier dry cabinets and floors, and the log confirms readings. See structural drying.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where the leak came from and whether it has been stopped.
  • Whether the cabinets are solid wood, plywood or particleboard.
  • What the floor is made of.
  • Whether the water reached the room next door.
  • Whether you have photographs of the wet cabinet floor.

If the sink cabinet is wet, remove the contents and leave the doors open. Air movement alone helps, and it keeps items from soaking up more water.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Closing wet cabinet doors and leaving them.
  • Running a dishwasher with a suspected leak.
  • Covering a wet cabinet floor with a mat or liner.

Hidden spots in a kitchen leak

Water from a sink, dishwasher or refrigerator line often travels under cabinets, behind the toe kick and into the subfloor. Pull the kick plate and look with a flashlight. If the cabinet base feels soft or the floor edge has lifted, the water went further than the surface shows.

Kitchens also hold items that make a wet loss messier, such as pantry goods and small appliances. Move food and cookware out early, and set aside anything that touched the water so you can photograph it for your insurer.

What affects kitchen cleanup cost

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Cabinet material. Solid wood can often be dried, particleboard often cannot.
  • Flooring type. Tile, vinyl, laminate and hardwood each respond differently.
  • Area affected. A single under-sink leak is smaller than a room-wide spill.
  • Drying days. Equipment time depends on readings.
  • Rebuild. New cabinet bases, flooring and paint come after the kitchen is dry.

Safety first and the standard we reference

When we judge wet cabinets and subfloors we use ANSI/IICRC S500 as the published reference for categories and drying.

  • Unplug small appliances only from dry ground.
  • Close the sink shutoff valves.
  • Keep food away from wet counters.

Questions about Kitchen Water Damage in Hollister

Can wet kitchen cabinets be saved?

Solid wood cabinets often can. Particleboard that has swollen usually cannot. A moisture check after extraction shows which is which.

How do I know if water is under my kitchen floor?

Look for lifting laminate, cupped boards or a musty smell near cabinets. A meter and thermal camera show hidden moisture.

Do you replace the cabinets?

We remove damaged parts and dry what is salvageable. Cabinet replacement and finish carpentry are rebuild work, which we can coordinate or hand to your contractor.

What should I do first if the sink is leaking?

Close the shutoff valves under the sink, empty the cabinet, and call dispatch if water reached the floor or walls.

Wall and Drywall Water Damage Repair in Hollister, CA

Wet drywall, damp baseboards and soft wall bottoms need drying or removal before they fail or smell.

Call (831) 271-0459

Wall and drywall water damage comes from a leak behind a wall, a pipe failure, a wet floor wicking upward or a roof or window leak running down a cavity. Drywall is paper-faced gypsum, and it softens, bubbles and stains when it gets wet. Dispatch answers 24/7 and we confirm availability for your address on the call.

This page is about the wall as the material. Ceilings, floors and cabinets each have their own pages, and we link them below. If a wall is wet right now, shut off the water if you know the source, move furniture away from the wall and call.

Signs your wall is wet

  • Bubbling, peeling or stained paint, often with a brown or yellow ring.
  • Soft or crumbling drywall at the bottom of a wall.
  • Damp or warped baseboards and trim.
  • A musty smell near a wall with no visible leak.
  • Wall surfaces that feel cool or damp to the touch.

Baseboards and wall bottoms

Water usually hits the base of a wall first, and drywall wicks it upward like a candle wick. A wet baseboard can mean a wet wall bottom plate and wet insulation behind it. We often remove a strip of drywall about a foot above the waterline, which is called a flood cut, so the cavity can dry.

That cut sounds drastic, but it saves the rest of the wall and prevents hidden damage. If a wet wall also has a ceiling stain above it, see ceiling water damage repair for how we handle both together.

Our process for wet walls

1

Map the wall

A moisture meter and thermal imaging camera show how far up and across the wall water has traveled.

2

Open and remove

Saturated drywall and insulation are cut out in straight lines so repair is clean.

3

Dry the cavity

Air movers and cavity drying equipment push dry air through the opened wall. See structural drying.

4

Repair

New drywall is hung, taped, textured, primed and painted once the studs read dry.

Plaster, old walls and local homes

Older Hollister homes may have plaster walls instead of drywall, and plaster behaves differently. It is heavy, it can hold water and it may need careful removal. If your home is in or near the Downtown Hollister Historic District, ask us on the call, and we will treat original plaster with extra care.

Painted walls with a stain after a one-time leak may only need drying and repainting, while a recurring stain means a leak is still active. If the stain appeared around a pipe wall, burst pipe water damage explains the first steps. If you see peeling paint without a clear cause, call dispatch and we can check it with a meter.

What to have ready when you call

  • Which wall is affected and whether it is an interior or exterior wall.
  • What is on the other side, such as a bathroom, kitchen or roof.
  • Whether the paint is bubbling, the wall is soft or the baseboard is warped.
  • Whether the source has been fixed.
  • Whether the wall has plaster or drywall.

If the stain is near an outlet or switch, keep the circuit off and ask an electrician to check it before you use it again.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Painting over a wet wall.
  • Pushing a nail into a soft spot to test it.
  • Leaving wet baseboards in place because they look fine.

What affects wall repair cost

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Wall area and height. Larger wet areas mean more removal and replacement.
  • Insulation and framing. Wet insulation is replaced, and framing is dried.
  • Source and category. Clean water is simpler than contaminated water.
  • Drying days. Equipment time depends on readings.
  • Finish work. Taping, texture and paint add to the repair.

Insurance in general terms: sudden leaks that damage walls are often covered, while long-term seepage often is not. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call (831) 271-0459.

Safety first and the standard we reference

Wall cavity drying follows the moisture mapping approach in ANSI/IICRC S500, which we use as a reference.

  • Keep power off to wet outlets and switches.
  • Do not poke soft drywall with tools.
  • Wear a dust mask if you open a wall yourself.

Questions about Wall and Drywall Water Damage in Hollister

Can wet drywall dry on its own?

Small damp patches can, but drywall that has been saturated usually needs professional drying or removal to avoid hidden damage.

Why is the paint bubbling on my wall?

Moisture behind the paint is pushing it off the surface. The source needs to be found and the wall dried before repainting.

How much drywall has to be cut out?

Usually only the saturated section, often a foot or two above the waterline. Readings show how far to cut.

Will the new drywall match my walls?

We texture and paint to match as closely as possible, though a full wall repaint gives the best look.

Emergency Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Hollister, CA

A failed tank can release dozens of gallons. Shut off the supply and power, then call dispatch for cleanup and drying.

A leaking water heater is an emergency when the tank has failed, because a full tank dumps everything it holds onto the floor. Dispatch answers 24/7. Close the cold water shutoff valve on top of the tank, turn off the electric breaker or set a gas heater to pilot or off, and keep everyone out of the water.

We clean up the water damage. A plumber or appliance technician replaces or repairs the heater, and the two jobs go best when they are scheduled together so a new tank does not sit in a wet closet.

Water heater leak: what to do right away

  • Shut off the cold water supply valve on the pipe feeding the tank.
  • Turn off electricity to an electric heater at the breaker, or turn the gas control to off or pilot.
  • Do not touch the heater if you are standing in water.
  • If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.
  • Photograph the tank, the leak location and the wet floor, then call dispatch.

Where water heater water goes

Heaters sit in garages, closets, utility rooms and sometimes attics, and the water follows the lowest path. In a garage it often spreads across slab and into stored boxes and the bottoms of walls. In an interior closet it can wick into the carpet and wall behind it, and from there into the next room.

Because the water is typically clean at first, there is a good chance of saving flooring and drywall if extraction and drying start the same day. Leave it for a week and the story changes, because water that sits against a wall bottom plate invites decay and odor.

Cleanup and drying steps

1

Extract

Wet vacuums and extraction units pull standing water off the floor. See water extraction for equipment details.

2

Map moisture

Meters check floors, baseboards and walls so nothing stays wet behind a surface that looks dry.

3

Remove wet materials

Saturated drywall bases, pad or insulation that cannot dry in place are cut out.

4

Dry and verify

Air movers and a dehumidifier run until readings return to normal, then the new heater and finishes can go in.

Hard water, age and what to ask your plumber

Most tank failures come from age and corrosion, and a leaking tank cannot be patched. Your water quality plays a part too. If you want to know how hard your water is, ask your water provider for its quality information, which is the City of Hollister Utilities division or Sunnyslope County Water District depending on where you live.

A plumber can also fit a drain pan with a line to a safe drain and install an automatic shutoff, which are the simplest ways to limit a future failure. If the tank leaked into a laundry area, our appliance leak water damage page covers the washing machine side. For a larger flood of the room, see flood damage cleanup.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where the heater sits and what is around it, such as carpet, stored boxes or a finished wall.
  • Whether the heater is gas or electric and whether it has been shut off.
  • Roughly how much water has spilled and how far it has spread.
  • Whether a plumber is replacing the heater, and when.
  • Whether the home is on a slab or has a raised floor.

Telling dispatch whether a replacement is scheduled helps us time the drying so the new tank does not go into a wet closet. If the old tank is still in place, keep the photographs.

What affects water heater leak cleanup cost

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Tank size and time. A fifty gallon tank that emptied fully affects more area than a slow drip.
  • Flooring and walls reached. Carpet and drywall cost more to replace than a sealed slab.
  • Where the heater sits. Garage, closet and attic cases differ in what gets wet.
  • Drying days. Equipment time depends on readings.
  • Rebuild scope. Cut drywall, replaced pad and paint come after drying.

Insurance in general terms: a sudden tank failure is often covered, while rust-through that leaked slowly for months often is not. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and keep the failed tank or photos of it. Want your real number? Call (831) 271-0459.

Safety first and the standard we reference

ANSI/IICRC S500 provides the reference for categorizing water from a heater tank and for measuring drying progress. We cite it as a published standard only.

  • Switch off the gas or power to the heater.
  • Close the cold water inlet valve.
  • Beware of hot water on the floor.

Questions about Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Hollister

Do you handle water heater leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Hollister?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7, and we confirm availability for your address on the call. Shut off the supply to the tank first.

Should I turn off the power to the heater?

Yes, at the breaker for an electric unit or at the gas control for a gas unit, as long as you can do it safely from a dry spot.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired?

A leaking tank usually must be replaced, though a failed valve or fitting can be repaired by a plumber. We dry the home either way.

Is the water from a tank clean?

Usually it starts clean, but it picks up dirt and contaminants as it spreads, which is another reason to start cleanup the same day.

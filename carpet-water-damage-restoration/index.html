Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Hollister, CA

Wet carpet and pad need extraction and drying fast. We tell you what can be saved and what cannot.

Call (831) 271-0459

Carpet water damage depends on three things: the water category, how long the carpet stayed wet and whether the pad underneath can be saved. Clean water caught within a day or two often leaves carpet that can be dried in place, while contaminated water means the carpet is replaced. Dispatch answers 24/7.

Carpet is a sponge on top of another sponge. The pad under it holds far more water than the carpet does, and it dries slowly. If your carpet feels squishy underfoot, stop walking on it, lift furniture to protect it and call.

Can your carpet be saved?

Water typeTime wetLikely result
Clean supply waterUnder 24 to 48 hoursCarpet often dried in place, pad usually replaced
Clean water that sat longerMore than 48 hoursHigher risk of odor and staining, removal more likely
Gray water (appliance discharge)AnyCleaning and sanitizing needed, often replaced
Contaminated water (sewage, flood)AnyReplaced, because porous materials hold contaminants

Why the pad matters

Pad is usually foam or rebond material, and it can trap water for days. A common approach is to lift the carpet, discard the pad and dry the subfloor beneath, then reinstall the carpet over new pad. That is cheaper than replacing the carpet, and it keeps moisture from sitting under it.

Carpet attached with tack strips can be pulled back, dried and re-stretched by a flooring contractor. If your carpet has a latex backing, it may separate when wet, which is another reason to check it quickly.

Odor and musty smells

A musty smell after carpet has been wet means moisture is still there, and the source of the smell is not the carpet alone. We dry the pad, subfloor and the base of nearby walls, and treat surfaces as needed. Health questions about indoor air belong to a physician, and we do not diagnose anything.

If the carpet was wet by a slow leak behind a wall, see wall and drywall water damage repair. For a bigger loss that included standing water, flood damage cleanup is the closer match.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where the water came from and how long the carpet has been wet.
  • Whether the water was clean or from a sewer, toilet or outdoors.
  • Whether the carpet is on a slab or a raised floor.
  • Whether there is a room or ceiling below.
  • The age and type of the carpet, if you know them.

Do not wait to see whether the carpet dries on its own. A carpet that is still wet after two days has a high chance of odor, and the pad is almost always wet underneath.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Walking on soaked carpet, which pushes water deeper.
  • Placing furniture back before the pad is dry.
  • Using carpet cleaners or steam on wet carpet.

Why the pad matters more than the carpet

The pad acts like a sponge under the carpet. It holds water long after the surface feels dry, and it is the main source of odor. A crew checks the pad and the subfloor with a meter, lifts the carpet where needed and decides whether the pad can stay or must go.

Carpet that was only clean-water wet and caught early is often dried in place. Carpet that sat for days or touched dirty water is more likely to be replaced, and a reading, not a guess, settles which it is.

What affects carpet restoration cost

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Water category. Contaminated water makes replacement likely.
  • Time wet. The longer it sits, the more likely the pad and carpet are replaced.
  • Area. More square feet mean more extraction and drying.
  • Carpet and pad quality. Replacement cost depends on materials.
  • Subfloor. Wet subfloor needs drying before new pad goes down.

Safety first and the standard we reference

Decisions on carpet and pad use the categories described in ANSI/IICRC S500, which we reference as a published standard.

  • Do not walk on soaked carpet more than needed.
  • Wear gloves if the water was not clean.
  • Keep power off to wet outlets.

Questions about Carpet Water Damage in Hollister

Do I have to replace carpet after water damage?

Not always. Carpet that got clean water and was dried quickly can often be saved. Contaminated water means replacement.

Is the pad always thrown away?

Usually, yes. Pad absorbs a lot of water and is inexpensive to replace compared to the problems it can cause.

How long does carpet take to dry?

Often two to four days with proper equipment, depending on the water and the carpet. Readings confirm it.

Can I dry carpet with a household fan?

It helps with a damp patch, but it will not dry soaked carpet and pad quickly enough to avoid odor.

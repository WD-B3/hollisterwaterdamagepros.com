Pipes and plumbing

Burst Pipe Water Damage

A pressurized supply line is pushing water through your home. Shut off the main, then call dispatch for cleanup and drying.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

When ice splits a pipe, the real flood starts at the thaw. Shut off the main and call dispatch right away.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

A failed tank can release dozens of gallons. Shut off the supply and power, then call dispatch for cleanup and drying.

Slab Leak Water Damage

A leak in a pipe under the concrete can soak flooring and spread beneath walls. Dispatch answers 24/7.

Appliances and HVAC

Appliance Leak Water Damage

Washing machines, dishwashers and refrigerators leak onto floors and into walls. Stop the supply, then call dispatch.

AC Leak Water Damage

A clogged condensate line or overflowing drain pan can stain ceilings and walls for weeks before anyone notices.

Kitchen Water Damage

Under-sink leaks, soaked base cabinets and wet kitchen floors need targeted drying before cabinets swell and fail.

Weather and building envelope

Ceiling Water Damage Repair

Stains, sagging and drips from above need attention fast. This page also covers roof leaks that reach the ceiling.

Storm Water Damage

Rain that got in through the roof, windows or walls needs drying before it spreads. Dispatch answers 24/7.

Flood Damage Cleanup

Rising water, heavy rain and groundwater need extraction, sanitizing and drying. Call dispatch any hour.

Basement Water Damage

Below-grade water needs pumps, safe handling and long drying. If you have a basement or lower room, call dispatch.

Crawl Space Water Damage

Water under the house soaks joists, subfloor and insulation. We remove it and dry the void from below.

Water removal and drying

Water Extraction

Standing water pulled out with pumps and extraction units, then handed straight to drying. Dispatch answers 24/7.

Structural Drying

Moisture mapping, air movers and dehumidifiers that dry walls, floors and framing until readings say they are dry.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Category 3 water needs protective gear, safe removal and sanitizing. Stay out of the area and call dispatch.

Floors and walls

Hardwood Floor Water Damage

Cupped, crowned or buckled boards can often be dried before they are replaced. Early drying matters most.

Carpet Water Damage

Wet carpet and pad need extraction and drying fast. We tell you what can be saved and what cannot.

Wall and Drywall Water Damage

Wet drywall, damp baseboards and soft wall bottoms need drying or removal before they fail or smell.

Commercial

Commercial Water Damage

Water at a business means lost days and lost stock. Call dispatch for extraction, drying and documentation.

How to choose where to start

If water is still moving, start with water extraction, since removing it is the first job whatever the cause. If water is gone but the room feels damp, structural drying is the step that gets moisture out of walls and floors. If sewage or flood water is involved, sewage backup cleanup and flood damage cleanup add disinfection and removal of porous materials.

For stains that appear days after an event, a ceiling or wall page is a better fit, and our hardwood and carpet pages cover floors. A business can start at commercial water damage.

A sewage backup is an emergency and a health hazard. Dispatch answers 24/7, and we confirm availability for your address on the call. Stop using toilets, sinks and washing machines, keep children and pets out, and do not try to clean it yourself.

Sewage backup: what to do first

  1. Stop using all water in the home, including toilets, showers and the washing machine.
  2. Keep people and pets out of the affected rooms.
  3. Do not touch the water or wet materials without gloves, boots and eye protection.
  4. Do not run fans, vacuums or electrical equipment in wet areas.
  5. Call a plumber to clear the line, and call dispatch for cleanup.

Stay out and stay safe

Sewage water is treated as Category 3, meaning it can carry harmful organisms. If the water is near outlets, an electrical panel or appliances, stay out and call the utility or 911 for any electrical risk before calling the restoration line. Health questions belong to a physician.

Water bubbling up from a drain, toilet or floor drain?

A backup means wastewater is flowing the wrong way, often from a clogged line, a blockage in the main sewer or a failed pump. The water comes up through the lowest drain, which can be a floor drain, a tub or a toilet. Because it is contaminated, porous materials that touched it usually cannot be saved.

Whether the blockage is yours or the public system's depends on where it is. In the City of Hollister, sewer collection is handled by the Utilities division of Public Works, and it can tell you whether the main is clear. The private lateral from your house to the main is generally the owner's responsibility, so call a plumber to camera the line.

Category 3 cleanup steps

1

Contain and protect

The crew wears protective gear and may set up containment to keep contaminated air and water from spreading.

2

Extract and remove

Water is pumped, and carpet, pad, insulation, drywall and other porous materials that absorbed it are bagged and removed.

3

Clean and disinfect

Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned and treated with disinfectants appropriate to the water category.

4

Dry and verify

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings confirm the area is dry before rebuilding. See structural drying.

Where sewage backups happen in older Hollister neighborhoods

In any older neighborhood, tree roots and aged pipe joints are common causes of blockages, and that includes older streets in a town with a historic core like the one in downtown Hollister. We are not stating a local backup rate. If your backup recurs, a plumber with a camera can show the cause, and the City Utilities division can tell you whether the main was involved.

Basement and low-lying fixtures are most exposed because they are the lowest drain point. If your backup reached a lower level, see basement water damage cleanup. For water that came from outside and rose into the house, see flood damage cleanup.

Some communities near Hollister, such as Ridgemark, have sewer service from the Sunnyslope County Water District, so mention who serves your line when you call.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where the backup came up, such as a floor drain, tub or toilet.
  • Whether you have already called a plumber or the city utility.
  • How far the water has spread and what it touched.
  • What the flooring and walls are made of.
  • Whether anyone has been in contact with the water.

If the backup is from the city main, the Utilities division can say so, and your plumber can confirm the private line. That distinction affects who fixes the cause, but cleanup should not wait for it.

Mistakes to avoid after a sewage backup

  • Running the washing machine or flushing toilets to see if it clears.
  • Cleaning with a household vacuum, which can spread contamination.
  • Keeping carpet or pad that touched the water.

Safety first and the standard we reference

For contaminated water, ANSI/IICRC S500 describes the highest category and what it means for porous materials. We use it as a reference for decisions on removal.

  • Keep children and pets out.
  • Wear gloves and boots, and wash well afterward.
  • Do not flush or run drains until a plumber clears the line.

Questions about Sewage Backup Cleanup in Hollister

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Hollister?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24/7, and we confirm availability for your address on the call. Stay out of the area and stop using water.

Why is dirty water coming up from my floor drain or tub?

That is usually a sewer or drain backup, where wastewater flows the wrong way through the lowest drain. Stop using water, keep everyone out and call a plumber and dispatch.

Who is responsible for the sewer line?

The city or district handles the main, and the property owner generally handles the private lateral. A plumber with a camera can show where the blockage is.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewage backup?

Often only with a sewer or drain backup endorsement. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

We do not recommend it. Contaminated water needs protective gear and proper disposal, and incomplete cleanup can leave contamination in materials.

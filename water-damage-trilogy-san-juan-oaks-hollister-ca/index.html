Where water damage starts in a newer home

  • An ice maker or dishwasher line that loosens under the cabinet.
  • A water heater in a garage or utility closet.
  • A toilet supply line or bathroom fixture.
  • An irrigation or hose bib line near the foundation.
  • A roof or window seal during a winter storm.

Extraction, drying and what you can do

Standing water is removed first. Then moisture is mapped with a meter and drying equipment is placed. Do not run household fans on contaminated water or use a regular vacuum on standing water. If water reached carpet, flooring or the bottom of walls, ask for a reading before you assume it is dry.

Insurance records

The insurer decides coverage, and policies treat sudden events and long-term leaks differently. Photographs, moisture readings and a written scope support your record. If you were away, note the date you left and the date you returned.

When ice splits a pipe, the real flood starts at the thaw. Shut off the main and call dispatch right away.

A frozen pipe becomes an emergency the moment it thaws, because a split in the metal or plastic can release water all at once. Dispatch answers 24/7. If you suspect a pipe froze, close the main water valve now, open faucets to relieve pressure, and keep everyone out of any room where water is spreading.

This page is about the water damage after ice has split a line, not about plumbing repair. A plumber repairs the pipe, and we remove the water, dry the structure and document the loss for your claim. We confirm availability for your address on the call.

Frozen pipe emergency: first steps

  • Shut off the main water valve before the pipe thaws if you can.
  • Do not use open flame or a torch to thaw a pipe. Gentle indoor heat is safer.
  • If a pipe is already leaking, treat it as a burst pipe and stop the flow at the main.
  • Photograph any frost, bulging or split sections before repairs.
  • Call dispatch if water has reached walls, ceilings or flooring.

How frozen pipes actually cause water damage

Ice does not usually burst a pipe where the ice is. Pressure builds between the ice blockage and a closed faucet, and the pipe fails in the section holding that trapped water. Damage often appears hours later, when the ice melts and the split starts to leak into a wall, attic or garage.

Typical trouble spots are outdoor hose bibs, pipes along exterior walls, attic supply lines, garages and any plumbing in an unheated space. Because the leak starts when you are not looking, water may already have spread to insulation and drywall when you notice it.

Honest note on how common this is in Hollister

Hollister is in the San Benito Valley, and we are not going to claim that frozen pipes are a constant worry here. How often freezing temperatures occur at your address is something to check with the National Weather Service forecasts for the area. What matters is that when a freeze does reach exposed plumbing, the damage can be severe because homes in milder areas are often not built to resist it.

Homes with pipes in garages, crawl spaces or exterior walls are the most exposed, and so are vacation properties where nobody notices a split for days. If you ever find a frozen line, the same drying steps used for other supply failures apply, and our burst pipe page covers the pressurized case in more detail.

Hidden water: attics, garages and exterior walls

Because frozen lines often run in places you rarely look, water may drip through a ceiling into the room below. If that happens, our ceiling water damage repair team handles the sagging drywall and wet insulation. In garages, concrete floors dry well, but stored boxes and drywall on common walls need attention quickly.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where the pipe is, such as an attic, garage, exterior wall or outdoor spigot.
  • Whether the water supply is shut off at the main.
  • Which rooms or ceilings are wet.
  • Whether a plumber has already been called.
  • Whether you have photographs of the split or the frost.

Because a split pipe often leaks only after the ice melts, the full extent of the damage can take a few hours to show. Tell dispatch if the pipe was exposed to cold earlier, even if it looks fine now.

What affects the cost of frozen pipe cleanup

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Time before discovery. A split that leaked for hours or days affects more materials than one caught fast.
  • Where the pipe was. An attic or wall line often wets multiple floors.
  • Water category. Clean supply water is simpler than water that mixed with debris or insulation.
  • Drying days. Wet insulation and framing take longer to dry than a wet hard floor.
  • Rebuild scope. Cut drywall and replaced insulation add to the repair phase.

Safety first and the standard we reference

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for water categories and drying goals, and it applies once a thawed pipe starts leaking. We use it as a reference only.

  • Do not use an open flame to thaw a pipe.
  • Leave the water off until a plumber checks the split.
  • Keep power off near wet ceilings and walls.

Questions about Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Hollister

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Hollister?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7 and we confirm availability for your address on the call. Close the main valve first, then call.

Is a frozen pipe covered by homeowners insurance?

Often, when the damage is sudden and accidental, but some policies add conditions about heating the home. Ask your insurer and keep photographs.

Should I thaw the pipe myself?

Gentle heat such as a hair dryer on a low setting is safer than any flame. If you see a split or active leak, close the main and call a plumber and dispatch instead.

How do I prevent frozen pipes?

Insulate exposed pipes, disconnect hoses from outdoor bibs, open cabinet doors on cold nights, and keep the home warm enough to protect interior plumbing.

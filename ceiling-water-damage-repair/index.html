Emergency Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Hollister, CA

Stains, sagging and drips from above need attention fast. This page also covers roof leaks that reach the ceiling.

Call (831) 271-0459

A ceiling that is stained, sagging or dripping is an emergency because wet drywall can collapse and bring insulation, water and debris down with it. Dispatch answers 24/7. Stay out from under a bulging ceiling, move people and valuables out of the room, and place a bucket under any active drip.

If the ceiling is bulging, a small hole made carefully at the lowest point can let trapped water drain, but only if it is safe to do so and no lights or wiring are nearby. If you are unsure, leave it and call. Do not turn on ceiling fixtures that are wet, and keep ladders away from water.

When a wet ceiling is an emergency

Treat any of these as urgent: a ceiling that bows downward, water dripping through a light fixture, a stain that grows while you watch, or a ceiling under a bathroom or roof leak after rain. Dispatch confirms availability for your address on the call, and calling early often means only part of the ceiling must be removed.

Roof leaks that reach the ceiling

Roof leaks have no separate page on this site, because a roof leak shows up as a ceiling problem first. Water enters through damaged shingles, flashing or a vent boot, runs along rafters, and soaks insulation before it reaches the drywall. The stain you see is often several feet away from where the roof actually leaks.

We deal with the water damage inside the home. A roofer repairs the roof, and a tarp or temporary patch is usually needed first if rain is still coming. We can dry the attic insulation, remove saturated drywall and document the damage, so your claim and the roofing work stay separate.

How ceiling water damage is repaired

1

Locate the source and moisture

A moisture meter and thermal camera map the wet area, including the attic space above.

2

Relieve and remove

Bulging drywall is opened safely, and wet insulation and ruined drywall are removed.

3

Dry the structure

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry joists, decking and remaining drywall. For the process, see structural drying.

4

Repair finish

Drywall is replaced, textured, primed and painted once readings show the area is dry.

Older plaster and finished ceilings in Hollister

Homes near Downtown Hollister and Monterey Street include older construction where ceilings may be plaster on lath instead of drywall. Wet plaster is heavy and can fall in sheets, so keep clear and let a crew handle it. Newer subdivisions usually have drywall that stains and sags, which can often be cut out and patched in sections.

For a wet ceiling that came from a pipe, our burst pipe water damage page covers the plumbing side, and the finished repair follows the same ceiling steps. If the wall below is also wet, see wall and drywall water damage repair.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where the stain or drip is, and whether it is growing.
  • Whether it rained recently or someone used an upstairs bathroom.
  • Whether the ceiling is sagging, bulging or has a light fixture in the wet area.
  • Whether the attic is accessible.
  • Whether a roofer or plumber has been called.

A ceiling stain that appears only after rain points up, to the roof. A stain that appears when an upstairs shower runs points to plumbing. That one detail often tells the crew where to look first.

What affects ceiling repair cost

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Source of the water. A roof leak and a supply line behave differently and may involve other trades.
  • Size of the damaged area. A single patch costs less than a room-wide ceiling replacement.
  • Insulation and framing. Wet insulation is usually removed, and framing is dried.
  • Drying days. Equipment and monitoring time depend on readings.
  • Finish work. Texture matching, priming and painting add to the repair.

Safety first and the standard we reference

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference we use when judging whether wet ceiling materials can dry in place. It is a reference, not a certification claim.

  • Stay out from under a sagging ceiling.
  • Turn off light fixtures in the wet area.
  • Place a bucket and leave the bulge alone.

Questions about Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Hollister

Do you handle ceiling water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Hollister?

Yes. Dispatch is available 24/7, and we confirm availability for your address on the call. Keep clear of any bulging ceiling.

Can a stained ceiling just be painted over?

Only if the source is fixed and the material is dry. Painting over wet drywall or insulation hides trouble and can trap moisture.

Do you repair the roof?

No. A roofer repairs the roof. We handle the water damage inside, including drying, removal and repair of the ceiling, and we document the loss for your claim.

Will the whole ceiling need replacing?

Not always. If moisture readings stay local, only the wet section is cut out and patched. A sagging or heavily stained ceiling is more likely to be replaced.

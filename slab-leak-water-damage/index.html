A slab leak is a leak in a water line that runs in or under the concrete foundation. It becomes an emergency when water breaks through flooring or you hear water running with everything off. Dispatch answers 24/7. Close the main water valve, and keep people off wet flooring near outlets.

Slab leak: what it looks like

Most people have never heard the word until it happens, so here is the plain version. Pipes that supply water sometimes run through the concrete floor of a slab-on-grade house. When one leaks, the water has nowhere to go but up through cracks, under flooring, or into the soil beneath the home.

  • A water meter that keeps moving when every fixture is off.
  • A warm patch on a floor, which can mean a hot water line is leaking beneath.
  • The sound of running water in silence, or a sudden water bill increase.
  • Wet or bubbling flooring, damp carpet or baseboards, and a musty smell.
  • Low water pressure with no clear cause.

Symptom first: why is my floor wet?

If the floor is warm or damp and no fixture is running, the cause may be a slab leak. A leak detection specialist can pinpoint the line with acoustic and thermal tools, and a plumber repairs or reroutes it. We handle what the water did to flooring, baseboards and walls, and we document it.

Safety around a slab leak

A hot water slab leak can scald. Stay off warm, wet floors, close the main valve and do not use electrical items around standing water. Call the utility or 911 for any gas smell or electrical hazard before calling us.

How we handle slab leak water damage

1

Stop the water

The main is closed and a plumber or leak detection company locates the line.

2

Standing water is extracted, and wet flooring, pad and baseboards that cannot dry are removed.

Hollister ground and slab foundations

Hollister has a known local factor: the Calaveras Fault creeps through town, and U.S. Geological Survey creep meters in downtown Hollister have recorded movement of roughly 5 millimeters per year. Visible curb and sidewalk distortion near Fourth Street and Locust Street has been documented. Whether ground movement is related to a specific leak is a question for a licensed plumber or a structural professional, not for us.

What we can say is that homes on slab foundations often have supply lines and drain lines buried in or under concrete, so a leak can go unnoticed. If you suspect one, call a plumber first for detection, and call us if flooring or walls are wet. For a leak from an overhead line, see burst pipe water damage.

Honest limits

We do not repair plumbing, and we do not diagnose foundations. A slab leak repair can involve opening the floor, rerouting lines or replacing pipes, and that is a plumber and often a permit matter. Ask the City of Hollister Development Services, or San Benito County if you live outside city limits, whether a permit applies.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where the floor is wet or warm, and what the floor is made of.
  • What the water meter does when every fixture is off.
  • Whether the main valve is closed.
  • Whether a plumber or leak detection company has been called.
  • Whether your water bill has jumped recently.

Slab leaks are tracked by a plumber, but the cleanup starts as soon as the water is stopped. If you are waiting on a plumber, tell dispatch so drying can be scheduled around the repair.

Common mistakes with slab leaks

  • Waiting weeks to act on a high water bill.
  • Covering a damp floor with a rug or new flooring.
  • Assuming a warm floor is a heating issue.

What affects slab leak cleanup cost

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Time of the leak. A long-running slab leak soaks more flooring and walls.
  • Flooring type. Tile, laminate, hardwood and carpet react differently.
  • Concrete drying. Slabs dry slowly, so drying days can be higher than for other leaks.
  • Reach into walls. Water that traveled up the base of walls needs removal and drying.
  • Rebuild. Flooring replacement is often the biggest repair item.

Insurance in general terms: sudden pipe failures are often covered, and gradual leaks often are not. Some policies treat the plumbing repair and the resulting damage differently. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call (831) 271-0459.

Safety first and the standard we reference

Drying under or around a slab follows the monitoring principles in ANSI/IICRC S500, which we cite as a reference.

  • Close the main valve if the meter keeps running.
  • Do not tear up flooring before the leak is located.
  • Keep electrical items off wet floors.

Questions about Slab Leak Water Damage in Hollister

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Hollister?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7, and we confirm availability for your address on the call. Close the main valve, then call.

Why is my floor warm and wet when nothing is leaking?

A warm, damp patch can mean a hot water line is leaking under the slab. Close the main, check the meter with everything off, and call a plumber or leak detection specialist, then call us for the floor.

How do I confirm a slab leak?

Turn everything off and watch the water meter. If it still moves, or you find warm spots, call a plumber or leak detection specialist.

Do you fix the pipe?

No. A plumber repairs or replaces the line. We remove water, dry the structure and document the loss.

Does insurance cover slab leaks?

It depends on the policy and the cause. Ask your insurer what applies to the plumbing and to the damage.

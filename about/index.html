About Hollister Water Damage Pros

A call-only crew for water removal and drying in Hollister and nearby towns.

Call (831) 271-0459

Hollister Water Damage Pros is a call-only water damage restoration service for Hollister, California and nearby San Benito County towns. You call one number, (831) 271-0459, and dispatch answers around the clock. There are no web forms, no booking portals and no waiting for an email reply when water is on the floor.

How we work

Every job begins with a conversation. We ask where the water came from, which rooms are wet and what the floors and walls are made of. Then we explain what the crew will do, which equipment it needs and what affects the price, before work starts. You decide whether to go ahead.

  • Water is removed first, because standing water spreads moisture fastest.
  • Moisture is measured with a meter so decisions rest on readings, not guesses.
  • Drying equipment is set up and checked on a schedule until the structure reads dry.
  • Materials are removed only when they cannot be dried or when they are contaminated.
  • Photographs and logs are kept so you and your insurer can see what was done.

What we do not do

We do not promise what your insurer will pay, because only the insurer decides coverage. We do not offer mold services on this site. We do not give plumbing, electrical or legal advice, and we recommend a plumber or electrician when the cause or the circuits need attention. Where a question belongs to another professional, we say so.

What a typical job looks like

A caller tells dispatch where the water is coming from and what it touched. We ask about the floors, the age of the building and whether anyone has turned off the main valve. That conversation lets the crew bring the right pumps, extraction units and drying equipment on the first trip, instead of making a second visit.

On site, the crew checks the water type, measures moisture and walks you through the plan. Extraction happens first. Then equipment is placed and readings are logged. Materials that cannot be dried are removed and documented with photographs. When readings show the structure is dry, the equipment comes out and you receive the records.

Working with insurers, landlords and property managers

Many calls involve a second party. An insurer wants photos and logs, a landlord wants a scope for the building, and a property manager wants to keep tenants informed. We keep records in a form each of them can use, and we leave decisions about coverage and responsibility to the people who own them. Our commercial water damage page explains how a business loss is scoped.

Standards we reference

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration, and we use it as a reference for water categories, moisture measurement and drying goals. It guides how we describe a job, though a standard does not replace judgment on site. Where a rule is set by a county or city office, such as a permit requirement, we point you to that office.

Questions to ask any restoration company

Ask what the plan includes, which equipment will run and for how many days, and what is excluded. Ask who handles the rebuild, and who pulls a permit if one is needed. A company that answers clearly on the phone is usually clear on site. We hold ourselves to the same standard, and we explain scope and price before work starts.

Places we know

Dispatch knows the roads, from State Route 25 toward Tres Pinos and Pinnacles to State Route 156 toward San Juan Bautista. It also knows the older core of Hollister, with the Downtown Hollister Historic District and Monterey Street Historic District, where plaster walls and older plumbing call for a careful drying plan. That local familiarity helps us ask better questions on the first call.

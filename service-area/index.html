Service Area Around Hollister, CA

The towns and ZIP codes dispatch covers, with notes on each.

Call (831) 271-0459

Dispatch is based in Hollister and drives to nearby communities around the clock. Below is where we work, with the ZIP codes we cover and a note about what is different in each place.

Hollister

We cover Hollister ZIP code 95023, which holds street addresses across the city, and 95024, the post office box ZIP. Our Hollister page explains conditions in the city, from the historic downtown to newer subdivisions.

Nearby communities with their own pages

Tres Pinos, CA 95075

Rural parcels, wells and septic, and creek-side lots south of Hollister on State Route 25.

San Juan Bautista, CA 95045

A historic core and older buildings west of Hollister on State Route 156.

San Martin, CA 95046

A Santa Clara County community north of the county line, mostly on wells and septic.

Hollister neighborhoods with their own pages

Inside and beside Hollister, five areas have local notes. See Santana Ranch for a master-planned community of homes and apartments, the Monterey Street Historic District for older homes, the Downtown Hollister Historic District for shops and offices, Ridgemark for an unincorporated community on the city's southeast edge, and Trilogy at San Juan Oaks for a 55-plus community.

Other places we answer calls

We also take calls from Gilroy and from rural parts of San Benito County. If you are not sure whether we reach you, call (831) 271-0459 and dispatch will tell you.

How dispatch reaches each area

Most routes start on one of two state highways. State Route 25 runs northwest from Hollister toward the Bay Area and southeast toward Tres Pinos and Pinnacles, and State Route 156 runs west toward San Juan Bautista and Monterey Bay. Reaching San Martin means State Route 25 and then US 101, across the Santa Clara County line.

We do not promise arrival times, because traffic, weather and what is already underway change them. What dispatch does is ask for your cross streets and any gate or road details, and plan the route and the equipment while you are still on the phone.

What changes from place to place

Inside Hollister, most homes are on city water and sewer, and building questions go to City of Hollister Development Services. Some Hollister-area customers receive water from other providers, so check your bill. Outside the city, many rural parcels use private wells and septic systems, and building matters go to the San Benito County Resource Management Agency.

San Juan Bautista has its own city government, so permit questions there go to the City of San Juan Bautista. San Martin sits in Santa Clara County, so its building matters go to that county. A well or septic system changes how contaminated water is identified, which is why our sewage backup cleanup page explains the difference.

Seasons and what they bring

The wet season through winter brings roof, window and foundation leaks across the whole range. Occasional cold nights can split exposed pipes in garages and exterior walls. Dry summer months hide slow plumbing leaks that only show up when irrigation or heavy use returns, and sometimes only through a higher water bill.

Rural parcels see extra problems such as failed pressure tanks, outbuilding plumbing and septic backups. Older buildings in Hollister and San Juan Bautista show plaster and old plumbing issues, while newer subdivisions show supply line, water heater and appliance leaks. Knowing which pattern you are in helps dispatch plan.

Landmarks that help when you describe your location

When you call, a nearby landmark helps dispatch place you quickly. In Hollister, people mention Hazel Hawkins Memorial Hospital, the Hollister Carnegie Library, the Veterans Memorial Building or Hollister Municipal Airport north of town. In San Juan Bautista, Mission San Juan Bautista and 3rd Street are the usual anchors, and in Tres Pinos it is State Route 25 and the creek-side streets near the center of town.

Cross streets work even better, along with the road type. A county road or private lane, a gate, or a long driveway all change how a crew approaches. A short description takes seconds and avoids a callback while water is still spreading.

Choosing between a town page and the Hollister page

If your address is in Tres Pinos, San Juan Bautista or San Martin, read that town page for local notes on wells, septic systems, older buildings and county offices. If you are inside Hollister, our Hollister page covers the city. Each page ends with the same 24/7 line, so you never have to decide before calling.

ZIP codes at a glance

Hollister uses 95023 for street addresses and 95024 for post office boxes. Tres Pinos uses 95075, San Juan Bautista uses 95045 and San Martin uses 95046. If your ZIP is not on this list, call anyway. Dispatch confirms coverage by address, and nearby rural addresses are often within range.

What to tell dispatch about your location

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Whether the property is on a private road, a long driveway or behind a gate.
  • Whether you are on a city water line or a private well.
  • Which buildings on the parcel are wet, including garages, shops and guest units.
  • Any road closure or storm condition you know about.

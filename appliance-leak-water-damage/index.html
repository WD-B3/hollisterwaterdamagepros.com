Emergency Appliance Leak Water Damage in Hollister, CA

Washing machines, dishwashers and refrigerators leak onto floors and into walls. Stop the supply, then call dispatch.

An appliance leak can look small and still be an emergency, because the water often runs under the unit and out of sight. Dispatch answers 24/7. Turn off the supply valve behind the appliance, then unplug it only if the outlet and floor are dry. If the floor is wet around the plug, leave it and shut off the breaker instead.

This page covers the appliance itself as the source: supply hoses, drain hoses, valves, ice maker lines and pans. The wet cabinets and under-sink area have their own page on kitchen water damage restoration, and we link the two because many calls involve both.

Appliance leak: first steps before you call

  1. Turn off the shutoff valve for the appliance, usually on the wall behind it or under the sink.
  2. Unplug the appliance if the area is dry, or switch off its breaker.
  3. Sop up what you can with towels, and pull the appliance out only if it is safe.
  4. Photograph the hose, valve and floor before moving anything.
  5. Call dispatch if water reached flooring, cabinets or a neighboring room.

Why appliance leaks are slow and costly

Many appliance leaks run for days before anyone sees them, because the water drips behind the machine or into the subfloor. By the time a stain shows, the flooring may be saturated, and the subfloor has soaked up the rest. A washing machine hose that fails while the house is empty can leak until the tub is dry, which is far more than a spill.

Ice maker lines are a quiet culprit, since they run behind the refrigerator where no one looks. If a stain appears on the ceiling below, the leak has come through the floor, and our ceiling water damage repair page covers that side of the damage.

How we handle an appliance leak

Extraction

Water is extracted from the floor, from under the machine and from cabinet toe kicks.

Moisture mapping

Meters trace the water through flooring and subfloor, and thermal imaging checks adjoining walls.

Targeted removal

Saturated flooring, pad or base cabinet panels that cannot dry in place are removed.

Dry and verify

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings are normal, and the written log supports your claim.

Prevention that actually helps

Braided stainless supply hoses fail less often than rubber ones, and replacing hoses on an age schedule is cheap insurance. A leak sensor with a shutoff valve at the washer can stop a failure the moment it starts. These are plumber or appliance jobs, and we mention them because we see the cost of the alternative.

In older Hollister homes with laundry in a closet or on a raised floor, water can travel through the crawl space before showing anywhere, so the crew may check there too. See crawl space water damage if water is dripping beneath the house.

In a master-planned community with both houses and apartments, such as Santana Ranch, tell us which you have, because water in an apartment can reach the unit below.

What to have ready when you call

  • Which appliance leaked and whether its supply valve is closed.
  • How long it may have been leaking.
  • What the floor is made of and whether it feels soft or lifted.
  • Whether there is a room or ceiling below the leak.
  • Whether you have the failed hose or part.

Keep the failed part if you can. An insurer or the appliance maker may ask to see it, and it shows the leak was sudden rather than a long-term seep.

Mistakes to avoid after an appliance leak

  • Pushing the appliance back into place before the floor is checked.
  • Running the appliance again to see if it still leaks.
  • Covering a damp floor with a rug or new flooring.

What affects appliance leak cleanup cost

Cost factors we explain before work starts

  • Time the leak ran. A slow leak over days reaches more material than a quick burst.
  • Flooring type. Tile can dry in place, laminate and hardwood often cannot.
  • Floors below. A second-story laundry can affect the ceiling below.
  • Drying days. Equipment time depends on moisture readings.
  • Rebuild. Replaced flooring, base cabinets and paint come after drying.

Safety first and the standard we reference

We reference ANSI/IICRC S500 for water categories, since washer discharge can be gray water while a supply hose leak is clean.

  • Unplug the appliance only if the floor is dry around the plug.
  • Close the supply valve behind it.
  • Do not run the appliance again.

Questions about Appliance Leak Water Damage in Hollister

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Hollister?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24/7 and confirms availability for your address on the call. Shut off the appliance supply first.

Does homeowners insurance cover a washing machine leak?

Sudden failures are often covered, but coverage depends on the policy and on how long the leak ran. Ask your insurer.

Can I dry the floor myself with towels and fans?

Surface water yes, but moisture in the subfloor and wall base needs meters and drying equipment to be sure it is gone.

Should I replace the supply hose?

If it is old, cracked or rubber, yes. A plumber or appliance technician can swap it for a braided line.

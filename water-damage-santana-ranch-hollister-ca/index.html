Water Damage Restoration in Santana Ranch, Hollister, CA

Water removal and drying for the Santana Ranch master-planned community, from single-family homes to apartment buildings.

Water damage restoration in Santana Ranch, Hollister, CA starts with understanding the kind of home you live in. Santana Ranch is a master-planned community, so one street may hold a single-family house and the next may hold an apartment building. Those two situations call for different plans, and a short phone call is usually enough to sort out which one you have.

Where Santana Ranch is

According to the developer, Santana Ranch sits at the northeast corner of the signalized intersection of Fairview Road and Hillcrest Road in Hollister. Hillcrest is a major road toward downtown Hollister and State Route 25. The community has no officially published boundary that we can confirm, so we describe it by those roads and by Rancho Santana School, a K-8 school marked as completed on the developer's plan.

Homes and apartments in one community

The developer's brochure describes the plan as approved for 1,092 residential units, made up of single-family homes and apartments. It lists five apartment communities by name: Village at Santa Ana, Ladd Ranch, Saddlebrook, Homestead and Bella Serra. The same brochure showed 715 completed lots, 63 lots under construction and 178 in engineering when it was prepared in 2024, so parts of the community were still being built.

We do not know the age or plumbing of any particular home here, and we will not guess. What we can say is that a development still under construction mixes recent homes with others that have been lived in for some years. The leaks we see in homes of any age tend to start in the same places.

First steps before the crew arrives

Stop the source

Close the valve behind the appliance or the main valve. If you cannot reach it safely, call the water utility or your property manager.

Mind the electricity

Do not stand in water near outlets or plug in equipment. Switch off power to a wet room only from dry ground.

Photograph first

Take photos of the damage from a safe spot before you move belongings.

Lift what you can

Raise furniture and small items off wet floors, and keep children and pets out.

How extraction and drying work

Standing water comes out first with pumps or wet vacuums. Then the crew measures moisture in floors and walls with a meter, sets air movers and dehumidifiers, and checks readings until the structure reads dry. Wet drywall, pad or insulation that cannot be dried is removed. Our water extraction and structural drying pages explain each step.

Documentation for your insurer

Whether your policy responds depends on the cause of the loss and its terms, and only your insurer can decide. It helps to keep photographs, the moisture readings and a written scope, and to call your insurer early. Renters should also tell the landlord or property manager.

Flood zone note

Questions about water damage in Santana Ranch

Do you serve both houses and apartments in Santana Ranch?

Yes. The plan differs for each. A house usually needs extraction and drying of the affected rooms, while an apartment may also involve the unit below, so we ask about the building before sending equipment.

Where exactly is Santana Ranch?

The developer places it at the northeast corner of Fairview Road and Hillcrest Road in Hollister. If you are unsure whether your address qualifies, call and tell dispatch your cross streets.

I rent in Santana Ranch. Who do I call first?

Tell your property manager right away, in writing, and photograph the damage. If they ask you to arrange drying, call us and we will explain scope and price first.

Does a washing machine leak need professional drying?

Not always. A small clean spill on a hard floor can often be dried at home. If water reached carpet, cabinets or walls, a meter reading tells you whether moisture remains.

Do I need a permit for repairs after water damage here?

It depends on the work. Ask City of Hollister Development Services before structural, plumbing or electrical repairs begin.

Water damage restoration in Ridgemark, Hollister, CA covers a community that sits just outside the city limits. Ridgemark is a census-designated place in San Benito County, next to the southeast edge of Hollister, and its mailing addresses read Hollister. That matters when you ask who handles permits, sewer service and building questions.

What Ridgemark is

The 2020 census counted 3,212 people in Ridgemark, in about 2.6 square miles. The Sunnyslope County Water District says it began providing sewer service in the early 1970s, when the Ridgemark Country Club was developed southeast of Hollister. Homes have been added over the years, so building ages vary, and we do not assume any one home's plumbing.

Typical causes of water damage in a home like yours

  • A failed supply line behind a washer, dishwasher or refrigerator.
  • A leaking water heater in a garage or closet.
  • A blocked drain or sewer backup into a lower-level fixture.
  • A roof or window leak during a winter storm.
  • A slow leak under a slab or in a crawl space.

For those, see water heater leak cleanup, appliance leak water damage, slab leak water damage and crawl space water damage.

Start with safety

Contaminated water

Keep people and pets out of water from a toilet, drain or sewer line. Do not use household fans or a regular vacuum.

Electricity

Do not stand in water near outlets or the panel. Call the utility or 911 for any hazard.

Shut off the supply

Close the main valve only if you can reach it safely.

Document

Photograph the damage from a dry spot before moving anything.

What happens next

A crew removes standing water, measures moisture, sets drying equipment and checks readings until the structure reads dry. Contaminated or ruined materials are removed and bagged. Water extraction and structural drying explain the steps. We explain scope and price before work starts.

Insurance records

Policies differ on sewer backup and on gradual leaks, and the insurer decides coverage. Photographs, readings and a written scope support your claim record. Read your policy for a sewer or drain backup endorsement, and ask the insurer before discarding large items.

Questions about water damage in Ridgemark

Is Ridgemark part of the City of Hollister?

The Census Bureau lists Ridgemark as a census-designated place next to Hollister, and it has Hollister mailing addresses. Ask San Benito County about permits and building questions.

Who provides sewer service in Ridgemark?

The Sunnyslope County Water District lists Ridgemark among the neighborhoods it serves for sewer. Contact the district about line or service questions.

A drain backed up in my Ridgemark home. What should I do?

Keep people and pets away, stop using the fixtures, call your plumber and call us. Contaminated water needs a different plan from a clean leak.

Do you cover homes near the Ridgemark golf course?

Yes. Call and tell dispatch your street or cross streets so we can plan the drive.

Do I need a county permit for repairs?
